# Government and Law Enforcement Request Policy

**Jimc Studio** · Sultanate of Oman
Adopted 13 August 2026 · Owner: the company's data protection contact (privacy@fizmoh.cloud)

This policy governs how Jimc Studio responds when a public authority — police, a
regulator, a court, or any government body, in Oman or elsewhere — asks us for
personal data belonging to our users, our customers' users, or data we have
received from Meta.

It exists because these requests arrive rarely and urgently, which is exactly
the combination that produces bad decisions. Deciding in advance what we will
do is the whole point.

## 1. Every request is reviewed for legality before anything is disclosed

No personal data is disclosed on a phone call, an email, or a verbal request at
an office visit. A request is acted on only when:

- it is in writing, on the authority's letterhead or official channel;
- it identifies the legal power being relied on;
- it comes from an authority with jurisdiction over Jimc Studio or the data;
- and its scope is clear enough to know precisely what is being asked for.

The company's data protection contact reviews every request against these
points before any data leaves our systems. Where the legal basis is unclear,
external legal advice is taken before responding.

## 2. We challenge requests we believe are unlawful

Where a request appears to exceed the authority's powers, to be unlawfully
broad, or to lack a valid legal basis, Jimc Studio will:

- ask the authority in writing to narrow or substantiate it; and
- where that fails, decline and, if necessary, contest it through the
  appropriate legal channel.

Declining is the default where legality is genuinely in doubt. Disclosure
cannot be undone; a delay can.

## 3. We disclose the minimum information necessary

We disclose only the specific data described in the request, for the specific
people named, for the specific period stated. We do not:

- hand over an entire account, database or export because it is easier than
  extracting the fields asked for;
- include data about other people caught up in the same records; or
- volunteer data that was not requested.

Where a request is broader than its stated purpose requires, we narrow it
ourselves and say so in our response.

## 4. Every request and response is documented

We keep a written record of each request containing:

- the authority, the date, and the legal basis relied on;
- the data requested and the data actually disclosed;
- the legal reasoning for disclosing, narrowing, or refusing;
- who inside Jimc Studio made the decision, and who approved it; and
- any external legal advice taken.

Records are retained for at least seven years.

## 5. We tell the people affected, where we lawfully can

Where the law does not prohibit it and no gag order applies, we notify the
affected user — and, where the data belongs to a business using our platform,
that business — before disclosing, or as soon as we are permitted to afterwards.

## 6. Data belonging to our customers

Businesses using Fizmoh control their own customers' data; we process it on
their behalf. Where a request concerns a customer's data rather than our own,
we direct the authority to that customer and notify them, unless the law
requires otherwise. We do not disclose a customer's data on their behalf
without either their instruction or a legal obligation we cannot refuse.

## Contact

Requests must be sent in writing to privacy@fizmoh.cloud.
